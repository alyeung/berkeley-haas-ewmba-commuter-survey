# EXECUTIVE MEMO: EWMBA Commuter Parking & Policy Changes
**TO:** Dean of the Haas School of Business  
**FROM:** Evening & Weekend MBA Association (EWMBAA) Officers  
**DATE:** July 12, 2026  
**SUBJECT:** Student Impact & Feedback on EWMBA Parking Permit Changes  

---

### Executive Summary
Following the recent announcement regarding changes to the Evening & Weekend MBA (EWMBA) parking permit policy, the EWMBAA gathered qualitative feedback from the student body. The response has been overwhelming and deeply critical. 

The feedback reveals that these changes are not viewed simply as a minor fee adjustment, but as a structural barrier that threatens **student safety, academic participation, mental well-being, and long-term brand equity**.

This memo categorizes the core issues raised by EWMBA students and proposes constructive, collaborative solutions to resolve this friction.

---

### 1. Key Feedback Themes

#### Theme A: Breach of Trust & "Bait-and-Switch" Sentiment
Many current students selected Haas over competing programs (e.g., Wharton SF, Stanford) based on the advertised benefits, which explicitly included parking permits to offset the time constraints of night school. Altering these terms mid-program has significantly damaged institutional trust.
*   **Student Quote:** *"When enrolling in the program, the parking was an expected benefit. Taking it away mid-program feels deceptive and unfair... like a bait-and-switch—joining under one set of expectations only to have those expectations altered once it is no longer practical to reconsider."*
*   **Reputational Impact:** Multiple students noted they would explicitly warn prospective applicants about this lack of support, presenting a direct threat to Haas's recruitment and program rankings.

#### Theme B: The Flawed Logic of "Program Alignment"
The administration's justification that this change "aligns" the EWMBA program with other MBA programs is seen as structurally flawed. Full-Time MBA (FTMBA) students live near campus and spend their days there; EWMBA students are working professionals commuting late at night from across the Bay Area (San Jose, Sacramento, Danville, Walnut Creek).
*   **Student Quote:** *"FTMBA students live closer to campus and spend the majority of their day in classes... Evening students must commute after their workday, and live scattered across the Bay Area. Given this, it clearly does not make sense to align these two programs on an issue such as parking."*

#### Theme C: Disproportionate Financial & Fee Burden
EWMBA students are charged mandatory student fees for campus amenities (gyms, recreational facilities, transit passes) they cannot realistically use due to their tight schedules (restricted to late nights and Saturdays). Forcing a new $500–$1,000/semester parking fee on top of these unused mandatory fees feels like "nickel-and-diming" the cohort.
*   **Student Quote:** *"Excluding a parking permit... while requiring students to pay an additional $125 per month for a parking pass... creates the impression that EWMBA students are being treated as second-class members of the Haas community and as a source of additional revenue."*

#### Theme D: Safety & Logistical Time Crunches
Public transit at 10:00 PM is not a viable option for many. Walking to the Downtown Berkeley BART station in the dark raises severe safety concerns, especially for female students. Additionally, sparse BART schedules (running every 30 mins) mean transit riders must sprint out of class, destroying the networking value of the MBA program.
*   **Student Quote:** *"Taking Bart back to SF after class at 10pm can be unsafe and tiring... BART trains Southbound leave every 30 minutes, which means I cannot network with classmates, professors, or guests at all for fear of missing the train and getting stranded."*

---

### 2. Proposed Compromise Solutions
We want to work with the administration to find a solution that respects the school's budget constraints while preserving the student experience. We propose evaluating the following compromises:

1.  **Restricted Class-Time Permits (Highly Recommended):**  
    Instead of full parking permits, issue EWMBA students restricted permits valid only during class hours: **Weekdays after 5:00 PM** and **Saturdays 8:00 AM – 6:00 PM**. This protects student class time while preventing any impact on daytime campus parking capacity.
2.  **Mandatory Fee Reallocation:**  
    Allow EWMBA students to "opt out" of unused student services (such as RSF gym fees) and reallocate those funds directly to offset their parking costs.
3.  **Post-Class Embarcadero Safety Shuttle:**  
    If parking cannot be restored, establish a dedicated Haas shuttle to San Francisco/Embarcadero immediately after class ends at 10:00 PM. This provides a safe, direct transit option during off-peak hours and increases the value of the mandated BayPass.
4.  **Reciprocal Cohort Benefits:**  
    If we are strictly aligning the student experience with other cohorts, equalize corresponding benefits—such as providing weekend dinner coverage (similar to weekday lunch coverage) or offering tuition discounts on summer courses.
